# Axx 0.22.19 · Audible personality and sound controls

- Fixed the voice sound controls so manual adjustments are added to the selected personality instead of freezing its EQ. Existing v1/v2 settings are migrated to per-control adjustments that keep their current audible values; the original settings remain in local storage.
- Made the personality audio profiles and EQ presets more distinct while keeping the output compressor after level gain. The level and pitch sliders now cover a wider range. EQ updates during playback; pitch and cadence apply to the next phrase.
- Added a personality selector in Voice Studio and distinct spoken previews. Juguetona and Psicópata · ficción +17 also have prepared, private greeting phrases. The phrase bank now contains 270 distinct entries.
- Kept Axxmorcito as the selected voice on this PC. The experimental CPU fine-tuned checkpoint remains separate; normal speech still uses the verified MOSS ONNX model and local reference audio.

## Validation boundary

Browser tests render the sound chain and compare bass, treble and level, and check that changing personality affects the preview and uncustomized controls. Native playback events and the installed app must be checked after packaging. Physical speaker listening and microphone-to-speaker timing still require acceptance on the target PC.
