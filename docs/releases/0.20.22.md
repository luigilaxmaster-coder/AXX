# Axx 0.20.22 — Phone control, commands and speech continuity

- Registered commands sent through the phone chat reach the same executor as
  Work. They do not need an AI provider. Results come from the operation record;
  unknown requests remain conversations. PowerShell/CMD and other sensitive
  operations still require approval on the PC, once per operation.
- Chat retries reuse the admitted operation. After PC approval, the phone can
  recover the final result without executing the command a second time.
- Avatar gestures use backend long polling to avoid background WebView timer
  delays. Movement accumulates against its target, so several packets in one
  frame no longer overwrite each other's distance. A stale PC pointer no longer
  immediately pulls the character back. Backend restarts reset the cursor.
- The PC selects the only currently connected phone for remote voice when the
  saved selection is disconnected. Voice retains its source classification:
  dictated shell scripts cannot be treated as typed commands.
- Android 0.1.4 (build 20) adds **Hablar con Axx**, Spanish/English selection and
  editable transcript confirmation on the phone. Android microphone permission
  and PC command authorization remain separate requirements. Avatar traffic uses
  the input transport queue, independently of chat/history.
- Custom PCM speech now refills its buffer after a late audio packet instead of
  repeatedly resuming with 15 ms of audio. Spanish technical initials are spoken
  explicitly (PC, USB, QR, CPU, GPU, CMD); displayed text and commands are intact.
  Original custom voice samples, identity and the English text path are preserved.

## Validation and limits

Targeted backend tests cover retries, query-only denial, sensitive-command PC
approval, result recovery, voice-session ownership and background avatar events.
Browser contracts cover real Web Audio scheduling, phone voice controls and
trackpad ordering. Android assembleDebug, unit tests and lint passed.

A shorter Spanish synthesis token budget was measured and rejected: it did not
consistently improve intelligibility and lengthened one voice markedly. The
current 100M custom speech model still makes pronunciation errors, particularly
with references recorded in English. No voice retraining or general naturalness
claim is made. Runtime/device results are appended after installed verification.

Review: implementing agent, without an independent reviewer. The Windows
installer remains unsigned. Private voice samples, pairing secrets and runtime
state are excluded from publication.
