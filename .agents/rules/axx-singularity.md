# AXX Singularity — Persistent Invariants & Delivery Rules

These rules protect the non-negotiable invariants of **AXX Project Singularity** (Windows 11 first) across all sessions:

1. **INV-01 — Two Presentations, One Runtime:** `FREE` preserves the 3D avatar (`SoftwareRenderer`), form/palette/finish/material, scale, orientation, living motion, and `OrganicTendrils`. `DOCKED` uses a separate, clean, compact, internally interactive notch surface. Never clone business authorities (`CommandService`, `ActionExecutor`, `ChatStore`, `ConnectService`, `NativeMusic`, `NativeVoice`, `MagicPen`, `Transfer`, `Agent` runtime).
2. **INV-02 — Zero Tentacles When Docked:** From the handoff into the notch shell (after retracting existing extensions) until valid restoration to `FREE`: zero `OrganicTendrils` draw calls, zero roots, zero segments, zero tendril hit regions, zero organic hover/click callbacks, and zero fake tendril decorations. Retracting existing extensions must never cancel the underlying business operation.
3. **INV-03 — Reversible Identity Snapshot:** Capture a revisioned snapshot of the `FREE` identity and valid position before entering `retracting`/`docking`. Restore the exact identity and valid monitor position on `undocking -> free` without writing transient animation states to persistent preferences.
4. **INV-04 — Action & Progress Integrity:** Consume real AXX capabilities through existing executors and contracts. Never invent handlers, permissions, results, or determinate progress percentages when the real source is indeterminate. Never auto-approve or auto-replay uncertain operations.
5. **INV-05 — Visibility & Escape:** Compact notch must remain recognizable without hover (unless explicitly suppressed by policy such as fullscreen/privacy/userHidden). Always provide an accessible path back to `FREE`, monitor switch, settings, and recovery (keyboard/menu/tray). Never intercept pointer clicks outside active interactive regions.
6. **INV-06 — Protected Baseline & Security:** Preserve `mobile/design-baseline.json` protected hashes (including `1.AXX/Axx.html#inline-styles`), `/v1` Companion compatibility, credentials, pairing, drafts, and pending operations. Keep the notch behind an opt-in feature flag until P0 gates pass. Do not bump desktop version or publish releases within this scope.
7. **INV-07 — Single Authority & Revisioned Geometry:** One coordinator owns presentation transitions; geometry and hit-test masks share monotonic `epoch` and `revision` numbers; stale acks and out-of-order events are discarded.
8. **INV-08 — Honest Evidence:** Distinguish `DESIGNED`, `IMPLEMENTED`, `TESTED`, `NATIVE_VALIDATED`, and `RELEASED`. Maintain `docs/notch/EXECUTION-STATE.md` and `docs/notch/TRACEABILITY.md` with exact SHA, commands, exit codes, and real artifacts.
